About Me

The story so far.

I'm a grad student at Georgia Tech pursuing a dual focus in Computational Science and Engineering and Quantitative Computational Finance. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, financial systems, and distributed data infrastructure.

I've built and shipped production data systems at Cloud4Robot in Seattle — a robotics-focused startup — where I worked as a Data Engineer Intern on cloud data infrastructure and pipelines. That experience taught me what it means to build reliable, scalable data systems in a fast-moving environment.
